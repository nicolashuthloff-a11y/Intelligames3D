Game Rules & Guide

Quarto 3D Gameplay

Quarto is played with 16 unique pieces possessing 4 distinct binary attributes: Tall/Short, Dark/Light, Square/Round, and Solid/Hollow.

Core Rule: You do not choose which piece to place for yourself! Instead, you select a piece from around the board and hand it to your opponent to place on the board. Win by aligning 4 pieces sharing at least one common characteristic.

Star Trek Tri-Dimensional Chess

Played across three primary 4x4 main boards stacked vertically plus four movable 2x2 attack boards suspended at the corners.

  • Movement: Pieces move along standard chess trajectories projected vertically across the stacked levels.
  • Attack Boards: Click any piece to highlight it, then click a destination square on any level or attack board to move or capture.
  • Objective: Checkmate the opponent's King across the tri-dimensional grid!
