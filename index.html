Intelligames Rules (Quarto, Twixt, Mühle, 4gewinnt)

🔷 Quarto Rules

Pick a piece for your opponent. Align 4 pieces sharing any common attribute (Height, Color, Shape, Top) across 1, 2, or 3 decks.

🌉 Twixt Rules

Connect opposite board borders with pegs and knight-move bridge links without crossing enemy lines.

🏛️ Mühle (Nine Men's Morris) Rules

Place 9 stones per player on the 3 nested squares. Form mills (3 in a row) to remove opponent stones. Reduce opponent to under 3 stones or block all moves to win!

🔴 4gewinnt (Connect Four) Rules

Drop chips into columns across 1, 2, or 3 vertical decks. Be the first to connect 4 chips horizontally, vertically, or diagonally in any direction!
