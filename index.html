Google Sheet Setup Instructions (Auto-Creates 6 Tabs)
  1. Open sheets.new in your browser to create a new Google Sheet on your Drive (name it Intelligames_3D_Sync).
  2. In the Sheet menu, click Extensions → Apps Script, delete any starter code, and paste the script below.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Select type Web app → Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone → Click Deploy.
  4. Copy the generated Web app URL, paste it into the field above, and click Save & Push Now. Your Sheet will automatically create tabs for Users, Sessions_Invites, Match_Scores, Move_History, Favorites, and Raw_JSON.
